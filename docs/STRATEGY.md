# TIMELINER: product strategy (proposal, 1 Nov 2026)

> Proposal by Claude for Nipanko. Nothing here is decided until Nipanko confirms it in PRODUCT.md. Prices, benchmarks and market claims are from memory: check them before relying on them.

## 1. The promise (one sentence people repeat)

**"See your time, play your day."** TIMELINER is the planner for people who lose track of time: one live timeline you can *see* moving, a buddy who cheers you on, and a game where showing up is what counts.

### Who pays first (pick one beachhead, grow later)

| Segment | Why they pay | Fit today |
|---|---|---|
| **Time-blind adults (ADHD or not), 18–35** | Losing track of time costs them jobs, grades and sleep; visual timers and planners for them are a proven paid category | Live now-line, visual blocks, forgiving streaks, Tick: **strong** |
| Students (17–25) | Exams, deadlines, study streaks | Strong, but low budgets; good for word of mouth |
| Indie creators / devs (like you) | Juggle many projects | Layers + goals fit well; small niche |

**Recommendation:** launch for **time-blind adults** ("ADHD-friendly", with no medical claims). Students follow naturally. Everything below serves that person: someone who wants to do the thing, keeps losing the hour, and quits apps that make them feel guilty.

### Why us, not the big names

- Calendars show *when*, not *how it's going*. Most planners are static lists.
- Habit RPGs punish misses, and people quit after the first broken streak (RESEARCH.md #10).
- **TIMELINER:** one live line from seconds to years, a companion with a personality, and a game that is **forgiving by design**.

## 2. Personality: Tick

A top product feels like *someone*. Ours is **Tick**: warm, a bit cheeky, always on your side, never guilt-tripping.

**Voice rules**
- Celebrates effort, not only results ("You started. That's the hard part.").
- After a miss: curiosity, never blame ("Yesterday got away from us. Want a 5-minute win to restart?").
- Short. Funny about *itself*, never about the user.
- Never shames, never threatens the streak, never uses fake urgency.

**Where Tick talks** (one line at a time, never a wall of text)
- Morning: "Let's draw today's line." Evening: "Best moment today: *Deep work, 90 min*. Close the day?"
- Streak milestones, level-ups, comebacks, first-time moments.
- A small "Tick says…" bubble on the Timeline, tappable for a tip.

**Tick grows with you:** cosmetic stages (sprout, then leaf, bloom, tree) unlocked by *consistency over weeks*, not by paying. Seasonal outfits (Diwali lights, exam-season glasses, New Year hat). Moods already exist (happy, proud, sleepy, focused, cheering).

## 3. The habit loop (what makes people open it every day)

The healthy version of "addictive": the app is worth opening because it *helps*, and it feels good each time.

| Moment | What happens | Why it works (RESEARCH.md) |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning plan (2 min)** | Tick asks for today's top 3; you drop them on the line. "Planned" fills the first part of the day bar | Implementation intentions #1, endowed progress #4 |
| **During the day** | Live line, next-up pulse, tap a node to complete: pop, XP, Tick cheers | Progress principle #5, goal gradient #3 |
| **Focus** | One-tap focus on the next task; park stray thoughts in the Inbox | Switching costs #7, open loops #6 |
| **Evening close (1 min)** | Wins first, best moment last, plan tomorrow's first task, +XP, streak +1 | Peak-end #19, implementation intentions #1 |
| **Weekly** | Sunday "season recap": hours focused, best day, new badges, a fresh-start nudge for Monday | Fresh start #12 |

**Daily quests (3 small ones, auto-picked):** for example "Finish one focus block", "Plan tomorrow", "Empty the inbox". Completing all three gives a bonus chest of cosmetic shards. Varied and small, never a chore list.

## 4. Streaks done right

Streaks are the strongest retention tool and the easiest to get wrong.
- **Forgiving:** freezes are earned (1 per 7 days, hold up to 2); a missed day uses one automatically. *(Built.)*
- **Repair quest** instead of reset: "Do 2 tasks today to save your 23-day streak." No reset-to-zero splash.
- **Milestones** at 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, 100, 365 days: Tick celebrates, unlocks a cosmetic, and the trunk trail changes colour.
- **Weekly streak** option for people who don't want daily pressure (calm mode).
- **Never sell streak repairs** to under-18s. Freezes are earned, not bought: a paid "save my streak" button turns care into pressure.

## 5. Progression and achievements

- **Levels** unlock cosmetics, never features people need: themes, trunk trails (lime, aurora, pixel, ink), Tick outfits, sound packs.
- **Achievements:** grow from 11 badges to about 40, in bronze, silver and gold tiers, plus a few secret ones ("Night owl: closed the day after 11 PM 5 times"). Each one rewards a *good* behaviour: planning, honest estimates, comebacks, breaks taken.
- **Seasons (monthly):** a free reward track plus a Pro track, cosmetic only. Fresh goals every month (fresh-start effect), and an easy story for marketing.
- **Loot without gambling:** completion "drops" are visible and earned. **No paid randomness, ever** (it's ethically wrong for teens and legally risky).

## 6. Make it genuinely useful (what people actually pay for)

Game layers keep people; usefulness makes them pay. The gaps a paying user hits first, in order:

1. **Recurring tasks and routines:** "Gym Mon/Wed/Fri 7 AM", a morning routine as a ready-made block of steps. *The* missing basic.
2. **Calendar import** (Google / Apple, read-only): meetings appear on the timeline as a grey layer, so planning happens around real life.
3. **Insights:** planned vs actual time, focus hours per layer, best hours of the day, "you underestimate study tasks by 40%" (RESEARCH.md #2).
4. **Widgets:** the live line and the next task on the home screen and lock screen (phone app).
5. **Templates:** "Exam week", "Deep-work day", "Weekend reset".
6. **AI day planner (later, Pro, opt-in):** "Here's my inbox and 4 free hours: plan it." Privacy-first: sends only what you choose.

## 7. Business model

**Free forever (generous):** timeline, tasks, todos, reminders, streaks and freezes, Tick, daily quests, focus, inbox, review, backups. Free must be good enough to love and recommend.

**TIMELINER Pro**
- Sync across phone and web.
- Calendar import.
- Insights and history.
- Routines and templates library.
- Pro season track and themes.
- Home-screen widgets.
- (Later) AI planner.

**Pricing to test** (regional; check against current competitors before launch)

| Region | Monthly | Yearly | Lifetime (launch only) |
|---|---|---|---|
| India | ₹149 | ₹999 | ₹2,999 |
| US / EU | $4.99 | $29.99 | $79 |

- A **7-day free trial** on yearly. The paywall appears at a moment of value ("Sync your 14-day streak to your laptop?"), never on first launch.
- **Payments:** App Store and Google Play in-app purchases on phones (their rules), Stripe or Razorpay on the web. **RevenueCat** keeps one "Pro" entitlement across all three. GST and tax notes go to the Second Brain vault.
- **No ads, ever.** Ads would break the promise and the privacy policy.

## 8. Guardrails (what we won't do)

- No dark patterns: no fake timers, no guilt notifications, no confirm-shaming ("No, I don't want to be productive").
- **At most 2 notifications a day**, chosen by the user; a quiet night window by default.
- Success metric = **days closed and tasks done**, not minutes in the app.
- Under-18s: no ads, no analytics, no paid streak items, no social features (already decided).
- Calm mode stays first-class for people who want less game.

## 9. How we'll know it's working

| Metric | Target to aim for | How to read it |
|---|---|---|
| Activation | 60% of new users complete a task on day 1 | Onboarding works |
| D1 / D7 / D30 retention | 40% / 20% / 10% | Top-tier for productivity apps |
| Days closed per active user per week | 4+ | The habit loop works |
| Free → Pro conversion | 3–5% of monthly actives | Pricing and value fit |
| Refund / churn | under 5% / month | People keep paying |

Measuring needs **privacy-friendly, opt-in, anonymous** stats for adults (LAUNCH.md §6), never for under-18s.

## 10. Proposed build order (one slice per branch, each approved)

1. **Recurring tasks and routines:** the basic gap; everything else builds on it.
2. **Tick's voice and the daily ritual:** morning plan, evening close with Tick's lines, tips bubble.
3. **Daily quests and achievements 2.0:** 3 quests a day, about 40 tiered and secret badges.
4. **Streaks 2.0:** milestones, repair quest, weekly streak option, trail colours.
5. **Cosmetics and unlocks:** themes, trunk trails, Tick growth stages and outfits by level.
6. **Onboarding:** the first 60 seconds (pick layers, plan 3 things, first completion) plus a landing page.
7. **Insights** (planned vs actual, focus hours, best hours).
8. **Pro and payments** (needs accounts live: the email setup).
9. **Phone app extras:** widgets, calendar import.
10. **Seasons** and (later) the AI planner.

**And alongside all of it:** a **closed beta with 20–50 real people** from your audience as soon as slice 2 lands. Their usage beats every assumption in this document.
